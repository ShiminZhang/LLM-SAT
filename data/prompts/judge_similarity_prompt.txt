2) Control structure similarity
- Are they both: single-threshold? multi-threshold? stateful controller? phase-based?
- Do they use the same states/variables and update cadence?

3) Parameterization vs idea change
- If B is basically A with different constants/weights, treat as very similar.

4) Expected qualitative behavior
- Do both aim for the same behavior pattern (more frequent early restarts, less frequent later, bursty restarts, stabilization, anti-thrashing)?

- similarity = 1.0: essentially the same algorithmic idea (minor rewording/tuning)

- similarity = 0.3: mostly different, small overlap (e.g., both mention "adaptive" but implement differently)
- similarity = 0.0: clearly different ideas
